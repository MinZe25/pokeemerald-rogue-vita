Encounter search

On the path screen a route's icon shows its type hint ("Typical" = Normal), and the way it faces shows the difficulty: Calm faces you (5 wild Pokémon), Average faces away (3), Tough faces left (2). The hint type always gets one of the slots. Percentages are the chance that one room of that kind has the Pokémon; charms and curses that change the encounter count aren't counted.
